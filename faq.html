faq

we know you have questions for us, and we'd be happy to answer them. we've compiled a list of our most frequently asked questions here. if you can't find the answer you're looking for, contact us and we will get back to you as soon as we can.

faq from adopters

how can i adopt a cat i see on catropolis?

we have a general form to request an adoption, which you can access by clicking here. alternatively, by visiting a cat's profile, you can click on the "adopt me!" button on that page. you can visit a cat's profile by clicking on their card on our meet the cats page.

what are the adoption fees for cats on catropolis?

we are not in charge of the adoption fees for cats on catropolis. each organization has set their own adoption fees for the cats under their care. however, we want to be transparent and have included a fee breakdown below.

life stage age range adoption fee range notes
kitten 0 months - 6 months $200 - $300 some organizations encourage adoption of a second kitten for a discount
adult 7 months - 7 years $85 - $200 some bonded pairs may come at a discount
senior 8 years and up $50 - $75

while we do list the adoption fee on a cats profile, we encourage you to contact the organization listed in a cat's profile for the most accurate and up to date adoption fee.

does adding a cat to favorites put them on hold for me?

unfortunately, we do not put any cats on hold under any circumstances. adding a cat to favorites simply makes it easier for you to come back to that cat. however, you may contact the organization listed under a cat's profile to inquire about holding a cat.

faq from organizations

how do i register my organization on catropolis?

you can access the form to register your organization with us by clicking this link.

how do i put my cat on catropolis?

you must first register your organization with us (which you can do by clicking this link). after submitting the form, we will contact you with the next steps, which include instructions on how to put your cat on catropolis.

how long will it take to get accepted as an organization

you can expect us to contact you within three business days of your submission of the form. if you do not hear back from us after a week, please contact our team at support@catropolis.com.
