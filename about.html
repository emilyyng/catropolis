who we are

we’re catropolis. our name comes from combining the words "cat" and "metropolis." metropolis in this case comes from the dc universe, and is the bright and hopeful counterpart to gotham, a dark and corrupt city. we want all cats to experience the hope and joy of finding a home, which is why we created this website and named ourselves "catropolis."

what we do

we help cats get adopted by providing a platform for shelters to spread awareness for cats under their care that need a home. we have tools to make the adoption process easier and as low-stress as we can. we encourage users to subscribe to our newsletter to stay in the know about cats new to the platform and cats that are still waiting to go home.

fun facts the creator loves about cats

  • if you're allergic to a cat, you're allergic to their saliva, not their fur
  • cats are programmed to know how to use a litter box (no training needed!)
  • cats only meow at humans, since they don't need to meow at other cats to communicate
  • if their head fits, their whole body fits because they don't have shoulders! (unless they're chonk)
